ABOUT

IEEE MTT-S MITS-DU Gwalior

IEEE MTT-S MITS-DU Gwalior is a student-focused technical chapter aimed at providing a platform for students to learn, explore and grow through technology and innovation.

The chapter encourages technical activities, workshops, seminars and collaborative learning experiences to enhance practical knowledge and build a strong technical community.
